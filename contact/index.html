Inquiry paths

Use the subject-line tag that best fits your inquiry. It helps me respond with the right context from the first reply.

BrandGuard™ build

Résumé Representative

You’re interested in GPT‑AJ01 — a conversational career agent that writes tailored résumés, cover letters, and LinkedIn copy on demand.

Subject tag: [Resume Representative]

Include: your current role / industry and a sense of the positions you’re targeting.

Professional Portfolio

You want GPT‑AJ02 — a living portfolio agent that turns your wins, projects, and strengths into structured, shareable narrative.

Subject tag: [Professional Portfolio]

Include: your field, the audience the portfolio is for (recruiters, clients, speaking engagements, etc.).

Enterprise Sleuth™

You’re exploring GPT‑AJ03 — an investigative knowledge agent for internal enterprise use cases: policy lookup, process documentation, or decision support.

Subject tag: [Enterprise Sleuth]

Include: a rough description of the knowledge domain and scale (team, department, or company-wide).

Website or GPT architecture

You want help designing or auditing the information architecture, prompt structure, or content strategy of a static site or custom GPT.

Subject tag: [Architecture]

Include: what exists today, what’s broken or missing, and the deadline if there is one.

General collaboration

Something else — a consulting engagement, a speaking opportunity, a podcast conversation, or a collaboration that doesn’t fit the buckets above.

Subject tag: [Collaboration]

Include: a one-sentence description of what you’re working on and what you’re hoping to explore together.
