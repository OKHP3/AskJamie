GPT‑AJ01: Your Résumé, Reimagined as an AI Agent.

Stop sending static PDFs. AskJamie™ — Résumé Representative turns your experience into a living, conversational agent that writes for you and sells for you.

Use our prompt-and-knowledge recipe to build a custom GPT that behaves like your personal résumé writer, brand strategist, and always‑on career rep — ready for every job, every recruiter, every conversation.

Talk with Jamie

Why turn a résumé into a representative?

Hiring has gone digital, automated, and noisy. Most candidates still lead with a one‑page snapshot. Résumé Representative treats your career as a system, not a document — so you show up as a whole person, not a keyword list.

From filter fodder to signal

Applicant tracking systems, templated job ads, and crowded inboxes mean most résumés blur together. Your agent translates your history into clear, tailored narratives for each role instead of one generic file.

Beyond “one more rewrite”

Traditional résumé services sell you a single artifact. This framework gives you a reusable engine: one core agent that can spin out countless résumés, portfolios, and talking points on demand.

Built for post‑COVID careers

Hybrid work, global applicants, portfolio careers, and AI‑screened applications changed the rules. Résumé Representative helps you frame remote work, cross‑disciplinary experience, and non‑linear paths with confidence.

What’s a custom GPT — in normal‑people language?

If ChatGPT is the general‑purpose AI, a custom GPT is like a trained specialist: same brain, different job description. You don’t need to code — you give it a role, feed it the right information, and set the rules.

A clear role & personality

We tell the model exactly who it is: your résumé representative, your tone, your boundaries, and how it should talk to hiring managers, recruiters, and to you.

Your private career knowledge base

Instead of memorizing everything, the agent reads from curated files — your experience, skills, quantified wins, industry language, and job descriptions. It reuses those pieces as needed.

A chat surface anyone can use

The output looks like a simple chat window. Under the hood, it’s a structured system that can draft résumés, cover letters, interview answers, LinkedIn updates, and talking points — all in your voice.

You’re not buying Jamie. You’re buying the blueprint for your agent.

GPT‑AJ01 is built on Jamie Hill’s own career as a live prototype. The actual offer is a repeatable recipe: prompts, knowledge files, and step‑by‑step guidance so you can build your own résumé representative.

Instruction architecture

A carefully structured system prompt that defines your agent’s role, tone, guardrails, and workflows — optimized for modern custom‑GPT behavior instead of one‑off “magic prompts.”

Knowledge starter kit

Pre‑designed knowledge files covering modern hiring, ATS‑friendly résumé structure, dynamic cover letters, market value and compensation framing, and interview prep patterns — ready to adapt to your story.

Build & usage playbook

Step‑by‑step instructions for creating your own GPT inside ChatGPT, connecting the knowledge files, and testing, refining, and safely sharing your agent with recruiters and collaborators.

Proof‑of‑concept included

This AskJamie™ edition shows the system in action on Jamie’s own data — so you can see how an integrated, multi‑decade career becomes a single, coherent AI representative before you ever plug in your own history.

Your secret advantage: job‑specific clones of your agent.

The power move isn’t just having one career agent. It’s cloning that agent for each opportunity — so every hiring team can talk directly to a version of you tuned to their role.

Tailored documents on tap

Feed your base agent a specific job description and company. It generates a hyper‑targeted résumé, role‑aligned cover letter, and a set of talking points that mirror the employer’s language.

A virtual agent for each role

Copy your base GPT, rename it for that job — for example, “Ask Alex • Senior Product Manager at Acme.” Share the link with your application. Now the hiring manager can ask your agent why you fit their role.

Multi‑dimensional you, 24/7

Instead of being a flat, two‑page snapshot, you become an interactive, searchable presence. Your clones can field questions, highlight relevant projects, and keep selling for you, even when you’re not in the room — or the time zone.

Saturate your opportunity space

Over time you can maintain a small constellation of agents — by role, by industry, or by career theme — each tuned from the same core recipe, each ready to be discovered, shared, and understood.

Why this beats “pay us for another résumé rewrite.”

There will always be résumé writers and career coaches. Résumé Representative doesn’t replace them — it gives you their superpowers on‑demand, without the recurring invoice.

Infinite versions, one investment

Once your agent is set up, you can generate as many résumés, cover letters, and interview answers as you like. No extra fees for “one more version” when a new posting drops.

Authentic, not generic

You stay in the loop. The agent drafts; you edit, approve, and refine. Your story stays grounded in facts, your voice, and your values — not in recycled templates.

Way beyond documents

Use the same system to prep for interviews, rehearse tough questions, redesign your LinkedIn profile, negotiate offers, and plan long‑term career moves. One agent, whole lifecycle.
