# QUALITY BAR
Do not stop at “good enough.”
The site should be:
- navigable
- coherent
- brand-consistent
- searchable
- indexable
- fast
- accessible
- legally safer
- internally linked
- documented
- maintainable
- ready to show publicly
- useful to a first-time visitor
- useful to a returning visitor
- credible as part of OverKill Hill P³
When faced with a tradeoff, prioritize:
1. No broken public experience.
2. No misleading legal/brand claims.
3. Clear site architecture.
4. Strong homepage routing.
5. BrandGuard showcase quality.
6. Search/SEO/accessibility.
7. Maintainable static architecture.
8.
 Visual polish.
Now perform the full audit and improvement pass.
