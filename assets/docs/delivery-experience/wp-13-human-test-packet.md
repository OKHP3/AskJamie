# WP-13 human usability and assistive-technology test packet

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: ready for human execution. No testers were contacted.

## Tasks

1. From the homepage, explain what AskJamie is and choose a useful next step.
2. Choose a lens, identify whether it is a prototype, and try or recover from
   its external GPT destination.
3. Find a named BrandGuard case through search and open it with keyboard only.
4. Use the contact page to identify the right inquiry context and email action.
5. Navigate the Universe without relying on the Mermaid diagram.

## Conditions to record

Run each task with keyboard only, at 320px or smaller reflow, 200% and 400%
zoom, reduced motion, JavaScript disabled, and blocked external resources.
Run spoken-output sessions with macOS VoiceOver and Windows NVDA when available.
Record completion, hesitation, confusion, focus location, spoken labels,
external access state, and any recovery action. Record automated Chromium checks
separately. Do not infer screen-reader usability from DOM or screenshot evidence.

## Acceptance

The visitor can reach the intended page or a clear fallback, understands the
prototype boundary before leaving, can recover focus after closing search or
navigation, and can reach meaningful Universe destinations without the diagram.
No conversion or business outcome claim follows from this packet alone.
